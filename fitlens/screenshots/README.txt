HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN APP SCREENSHOTS
==================================

1. Take screenshots of the real Fit Lens app on your phone.
2. Name them  1.png, 2.png, 3.png, 4.png, ...  (numbered, in order)
3. Put them in THIS folder (screenshots/), replacing the placeholders.
4. List them in the SCREENS list at the bottom of /index.html
   (find the line that says  var SCREENS = [ ... ] )
   — add one line per image. You can add as many as you want; the
   carousel updates automatically. Missing images are skipped.

Tips:
- Use tall portrait screenshots (roughly 9:19.5, e.g. 1080x2340).
- PNG or JPG both work.
- The first image is shown first.
